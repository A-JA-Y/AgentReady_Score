Rafael Torres

Founder & CTO · AgentReady

Rafael Torres is a web infrastructure engineer who spent a decade at Cloudflare and Fastly working on web standards and crawling protocols before the AI agent revolution made his passion project urgent. With a background in distributed systems and machine-readable content, he saw the emergence of AI agents as a fundamental shift in how the web would be accessed.

"Most website owners have no idea their site is effectively invisible to AI agents. The businesses that figure this out first will have a compounding advantage — just like early SEO adopters did in 2005."

He is a contributor to the llms.txt specification, a member of the W3C Web Agents working group, and a frequent speaker at WebSummit and O'Reilly Velocity.
